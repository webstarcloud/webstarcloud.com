- primitives, constraints, invariants, guardrails, irreversible decisions

- signals, drift, containment, recovery
- calm, precise, evidence‑driven language

Avoid:
- hype, “revolutionary,” “magic,” “game‑changing”
- casual fluff, memes, or trend‑chasing
- product marketing tone
- tool‑first descriptions without system context
