# Governance — Brand System (v1)

## Weekly Review (15 min)
- What shipped?
- Did it reinforce the thesis?
- What should be cut?

## Monthly Pruning
- Archive off‑brand content
- Update “current explorations”

## Quarterly Refresh
- Re‑evaluate pillars
- Promote strongest work
- Retire weak signals

## Pre‑Ship North Star
- Reinforces “R&D‑forward systems architect”
- Signals “builder of primitives”
- 30‑second clarity for a future co‑founder / CTO / investor

If any answer is “no,” do not ship.
