Selected System Work

Three systems. High-stakes constraints. Clear architectural shifts.

Pillars: Systems + Primitives + Judgement

TMNL privacy-preserving analytics (sanitised)

Constraint: sensitive mobility data could not move outside strict access boundaries.

Primitive: access-bounded streams with invariant checks on every transformation edge.

Outcome: analytics stayed useful while reducing leakage pathways and review cycles.

What changed: privacy became runtime behavior instead of an after-the-fact control.

Standard Bank real-time event platform recovery (sanitised)

Constraint: transaction pathways needed recovery under tight RTO and audit pressure.

Primitive: schema contracts plus rehearsed recovery paths at stream boundaries.

Outcome: faster incident containment with less schema drift under peak load.

What changed: moved from reactive recovery to contract-led operational discipline.

GPU digital twin platform redesign (sanitised)

Constraint: model fidelity had to hold while GPU contention and workload drift increased.

Primitive: isolated compute lanes with explicit containment and truth-table checks.

Outcome: steadier simulation throughput and clearer escalation boundaries.

What changed: control-plane decisions moved ahead of scaling decisions.
