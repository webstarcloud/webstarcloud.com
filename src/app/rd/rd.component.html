R&D

Experiments under constraints.

R&D here is not a product backlog. It is a lab for primitives: narrow experiments that force hard architectural choices early, so the system can survive irreversible decisions later.

Flagship artifacts

Each artifact states the question, the primitive it isolates, why it matters, and status.

Holodeck

Question: How do we simulate production constraints before the first migration?

Primitive: deterministic environment playback and reversible state shards.

Why it matters at scale: allows irreversible changes to be rehearsed under real load.

Status: Active

Radar

Question: Can drift signals be detected before they become outages?

Primitive: multi-signal anomaly fusion with tiered confidence gates.

Why it matters at scale: catches weak signals early when rollbacks are still possible.

Status: Active

Invariant Ledger

Question: How do we make architectural invariants explicit and enforceable?

Primitive: policy-bound data contracts with drift detection at the edge.

Why it matters at scale: prevents silent erosion of guarantees across teams.

Status: Experimental

Failsafe Mesh

Question: What is the minimum viable containment layer for agentic systems?

Primitive: guardrail orchestration with explicit blast-radius limits.

Why it matters at scale: keeps autonomy safe under adversarial and unknown inputs.

Status: Experimental
