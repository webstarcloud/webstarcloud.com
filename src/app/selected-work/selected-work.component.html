Credibility spine.

A subset of high-stakes systems where correctness, security, and irreversible decisions are the real constraints. Details are intentionally architectural and sanitized.

Homomorphic Encryption Systems

Problem framing: compute on sensitive data without exposing it at rest or in use.

Constraints: latency ceilings, cryptographic correctness, and provable auditability.

Architectural insight: separate key orchestration from compute pipelines to contain blast radius.

What this changed: treat privacy guarantees as product behavior, not a bolt-on layer.

TMNL Platform Rebuild (World-First)

Problem framing: rebuild a live platform with zero rollback tolerance.

Constraints: continuous service, hard deadlines, and irreversible data migration paths.

Architectural insight: migration truth tables + parallel-validated compute to prevent silent drift.

What this changed: always design for irreversible moves before a single line ships.

High-Stakes Production Controls

Problem framing: guard critical workloads where downtime is a direct business event.

Constraints: audit requirements, shared tenancy, and adversarial traffic patterns.

Architectural insight: enforce invariant-led controls at ingress rather than post-facto analysis.

What this changed: reliability becomes a policy surface, not just an SRE function.
