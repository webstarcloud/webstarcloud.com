TMNL: access-bounded streams + invariants

System: privacy-preserving analytics platform for mobility intelligence.

Constraint: sensitive datasets could not be copied or broadened beyond access boundaries.

Primitive: stream-level invariants with access-bounded contracts on transformation edges.

Outcome: useful analytics with smaller blast radius and lower compliance friction.

What changed: privacy shifted from policy interpretation to enforceable runtime behavior.

Sanitised notes: architecture and sequencing are retained; client-specific internals are removed.

Standard Bank: schema contracts + drift prevention

System: real-time event platform recovery and resilience hardening.

Constraint: strict recovery objectives under continuous transactional demand.

Primitive: schema contracts and drift gates at stream ingress and handoff boundaries.

Outcome: improved containment and faster restoration during failure windows.

What changed: recovery paths became designed assets, not emergency improvisation.

Sanitised notes: timings and internal service names are abstracted; decision patterns are preserved.

Backbase: control plane + governance boundaries for agentic systems

System: agentic workflow control plane for regulated financial product delivery.

Constraint: autonomy needed strict governance boundaries, auditability, and escalation routes.

Primitive: policy-gated control plane with explicit containment and handoff contracts.

Outcome: safer experimentation with clearer ownership across platform and domain teams.

What changed: governance moved upstream into orchestration primitives.

Sanitised notes: implementation specifics are generalized while boundary design remains explicit.
