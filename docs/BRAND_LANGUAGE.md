# Brand Language (Internal)

## Canonical pillars

- `R&D`: Probe uncertain design space early with measurable experiments before committing at scale.
- `Systems`: Design end-to-end behavior across services, teams, and operational boundaries.
- `Primitives`: Build small, durable building blocks that hold under stress and composition.
- `Judgement`: Make tradeoffs explicit with clear constraints, recovery paths, and accountability.

## Phrase rotation list

Use these terms to avoid repeating a single phrase pattern:

- invariants
- containment
- recovery paths
- blast radius
- auditability
- drift
